Pork Steak

Homemade one-skillet meal featuring tender pork butt steaks cooked in soy sauce and green onions

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup butter
  • ¼ cup soy sauce
  • 1 bunch green onions, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 pork butt steaks

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat; stir in soy sauce
  3. Add green onions and garlic; cook and stir until lightly browned, about 2 minutes
  4. Place pork steaks into the skillet. Cover and cook until browned, 8 to 10 minutes per side
  5. Remove the lid and continue cooking until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest part of a steak reads 145 degrees F (63 degrees C), about 10 minutes.
  6. Enjoy!
